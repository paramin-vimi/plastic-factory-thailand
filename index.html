FAQ

Our factories, answered.

Question we have not covered? Ask our team →

Where exactly are your plastic factories in Thailand?

We run two plants. Our flagship is Alcami Manufacturing at 693 Moo 10 Tha-Tum, Industrial Park 304, Srimahaphote 25140, Prachinburi. Our second site is Alcami Industries at 64/189 Moo 4, Eastern Seaboard Industrial Estate, Pluakdaeng, Rayong 21140. The group also runs a plant in the Philippines and a sourcing office in Singapore.

How big are the factories, and how many people work there?

Prachinburi has around 10,000 m² of factory floor and approximately 200 people, on a 22,500 m² plot with room to expand. Rayong has around 6,400 m² of floor and approximately 80 people, on an 8,000 m² plot. That is roughly 16,400 m² of production space in Thailand.

Can I see the factory without flying to Thailand?

Yes — the 360° virtual tour walks you through the Prachinburi plant scene by scene: entrance and lobby, thermoforming, the injection room, the cleanroom, washing, machining, high-speed CNC and the test lab. It runs in your browser, with no signup.

Which processes run at which plant?

Prachinburi runs thermoforming, injection molding, DI washing and CNC machining, and it is the site equipped for cleanroom production. Rayong runs sheet extrusion, film conversion and warehousing. If your part needs several of these, they are coordinated inside one group rather than across separate suppliers.

What machines do you actually have?

18 injection molding machines spanning a 10- to 180-ton range, seven pressure forming machines alongside one vacuum former, co-extrusion lines feeding a single die, and 3-axis CNC machining with milling centers and lathes in a range of sizes. It is a real spread, but a finite one — so we will tell you honestly if your part sits outside it.

Do the factories have cleanrooms?

Yes. We form and mold in Class 10,000 / ISO 7 cleanrooms at Prachinburi, wash parts using our own deionized water plant at 16 MΩ resistivity filtered to 0.1 micron, and finish with cleanroom packing for parts that must arrive clean.

What testing can you do on site?

Our in-house lab covers UV chamber, weatherability, drop, vibration, salt spray and ESD testing, plus CMM measurement for dimensional verification and LPC, FTIR and IC testing for cleanliness. Testing your parts in the same building that made them removes a round trip to an external lab.

Are the factories certified?

The group holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 22000. Prachinburi is the plant equipped for cleanroom production, so cleanliness-critical work runs there. Ask and we will send certificate copies for your supplier qualification file.

How do parts get from your factory to us?

Finished goods go into our own warehouses and leave on our own trucks, so there is no third-party handover between the line and the loading bay. Rayong sits on the Eastern Seaboard, a short drive from Laem Chabang port and from Bangkok. Export and duty paperwork, including certificates of origin, is handled at group level — tell us your destination market and we will confirm what we can file for you.
